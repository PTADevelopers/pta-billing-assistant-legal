Launching the PTA Billing Assistant

Internal application · Prime Team Agency

The PTA Billing Assistant is an internal tool for Prime Team Agency. It runs inside PTA’s private Slack workspace — authorized team members use it by messaging the assistant directly in Slack. There is no separate public sign-in.

To use it: allowlisted PTA staff open a direct message with the PTA Billing Assistant in Slack and ask about overdue invoices, client revenue, or ad spend. Access is limited to named PTA personnel.

To request access or ask a question, contact developers@primeteam.agency.
