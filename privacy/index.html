PTA Billing Assistant — Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the PTA Billing Assistant (the “Application”) accesses, how Prime Team Agency (“PTA,” “we,” or “us”) uses it, and the choices available to the people whose data it touches. The Application is an internal tool used only by authorized PTA personnel to support billing and accounts-receivable operations.

1 Who this covers

The Application is not a public product. It is operated by PTA for its own staff and runs inside PTA’s private Slack workspace. Access is restricted to named, allowlisted PTA employees. “You” means an authorized PTA user of the Application.

2 Information we access

The Application reads business data from connected systems in order to answer billing questions. It does not create profiles of individuals for advertising and does not collect data from the general public. Specifically, it may access:

  • QuickBooks Online data (via Intuit): invoices, customer records, accounts-receivable and aging reports, sales figures, and company information — read-only.
  • MerchantSpring data: marketplace sales, advertising spend, and profit-and-loss figures used for client revenue and ad-spend reporting — read-only.
  • Slack messages sent to the Application by authorized users, and the identity of the requesting user, in order to respond.

3 How we use it

We use the information solely to operate the Application’s stated functions for PTA’s internal use:

  • Identifying overdue invoices and summarizing accounts receivable;
  • Reporting client revenue and advertising spend over requested periods;
  • Drafting follow-up email text for a PTA teammate to review and send.

We do not use this information for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to build or train third-party advertising or profiling products.

4 QuickBooks and Intuit data

The Application accesses your QuickBooks Online company data through Intuit’s official APIs, using read-only access. We request only the accounting scope necessary to read invoices, customers, and receivables reports. QuickBooks data is used only for the internal billing and reporting purposes described in this policy, is never sold or shared for advertising, and is handled in accordance with Intuit’s applicable requirements. You can revoke the Application’s access at any time from your Intuit account’s connected-apps settings.

5 How the information is shared

We do not sell personal or financial information. We share it only with the limited service providers that make the Application function, each acting on our instructions:

  • Cloud hosting — the infrastructure the Application runs on;
  • AI model provider — the language-model service that generates the Application’s responses from the data it is given;
  • Slack — the messaging platform used to deliver requests and responses.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect PTA’s rights.

6 Retention

The Application queries connected systems on demand and keeps working records (such as conversation history) only as long as needed to operate the tool and meet PTA’s recordkeeping and legal obligations. Source financial records remain in QuickBooks Online and MerchantSpring; the Application is a reader, not the system of record.

7 Security

Access is limited to allowlisted PTA users. Credentials for connected services are stored with restricted file permissions and are not exposed in the Application’s responses. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to internal financial data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Your choices

Authorized users and PTA administrators may request that access to a connected system be revoked, that stored working data be deleted, or that questions about this policy be answered, by contacting us at the address below. Connections to Intuit and other services can also be disconnected directly in those services’ settings.

9 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Application evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page.

10 Contact

Questions about this policy or the data the Application handles can be directed to Prime Team Agency at developers@primeteam.agency.
