Connect to QuickBooks Online

Authorize read-only access

The PTA Billing Assistant connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit’s secure OAuth 2.0 authorization. It requests read-only access to invoices, customers, and accounts-receivable reports, and never creates, edits, or deletes accounting records.

A Prime Team Agency administrator authorizes the connection once, from within Intuit, by granting the Accounting scope to the application. The connection can be revoked at any time — see Disconnect.

If you are a PTA administrator and need to establish or re-establish the QuickBooks connection, contact developers@primeteam.agency to begin the authorization.
